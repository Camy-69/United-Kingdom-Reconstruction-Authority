Programme aims

  • Reduce congestion, improve reliability and make movement more accessible for communities and businesses.
  • Improve the safety and quality of rail, roads, bus networks and active travel infrastructure.
  • Support economic growth by connecting people to jobs, services and housing.
  • Build transport systems that are more climate resilient and capable of long-term maintenance.

Priority areas

  • RailHigh
  • BusesHigh
  • Active travelHigh

Core transport interventions

  • Rail and intercity links — reliability upgrades, station modernisation and network resilience.
  • Local public transport — bus priority, integrated ticketing and service reliability improvements.
  • Active travel and public realm — cycle routes, walking corridors, junction upgrades and safer streets.
  • Road and freight network — corridor management, maintenance and freight movement improvements.
