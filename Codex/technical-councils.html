Technical Councils

Seven specialised councils provide technical oversight, standards, cost assurance and delivery coordination across the national reconstruction programme.

Mandate

  • Set and maintain technical standards for infrastructure, housing, energy and public services.
  • Review investment quality, delivery risk and lifecycle performance across each portfolio.
  • Coordinate regional and national priorities so projects align with approved programme outcomes.
  • Deliver transparent decision support for ministers, local government, delivery partners and public scrutiny.

Core governance model

  • Programme assuranceQuarterly
  • Standards reviewMonthly
  • Regional alignmentBi-quarterly
  • Public reportingOpen briefings

Council structure

  • Housing & Living Standards Council — performance, accessibility, affordability and low-carbon dwelling standards.
  • Infrastructure & Utilities Council — water, roads, sewerage, fibre and essential civic services.
  • Transport & Mobility Council — rail, buses, active travel, junction design and accessible movement.
  • Energy & Climate Council — grid resilience, decarbonisation, heat, renewables and flood adaptation.
  • Digital & Data Council — public dashboards, interoperability, digital standards and operational reporting.

Priority themes

  • ResilienceHigh

Operational outputs

  • Review and update national design standards and technical guidance.
  • Assess the readiness of project pipelines before procurement and contract award.
  • Coordinate national and regional evidence, datasets and performance reporting.
  • Provide technical briefs to support local authorities, delivery bodies and public accountability.
