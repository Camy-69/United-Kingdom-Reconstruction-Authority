Leeds

Leeds City Square is being transformed into a people-first civic heart with cleaner mobility, higher-quality public space and greener neighbourhood infrastructure.

Our vision

  • Leeds City Square becomes a world-class, people-first city centre with public space, green corridors and climate-ready streets.
  • Prioritise active travel, clean air and durable public transport to improve everyday access.
  • Deliver high-quality housing, resilient infrastructure and better local jobs in the city centre and surrounding districts.
  • Support a thriving economy through investment in skills, public services and low-carbon growth.

  • Bus priority, cycling links and accessible connections to employment hubs.
