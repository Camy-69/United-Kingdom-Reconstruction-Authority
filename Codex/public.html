Public-facing goals

  • Reconstruction should improve everyday life, not just asset replacement or programme completion.
  • Communities need clear information, direct engagement and visible accountability on local outcomes.
  • Public services should be more reliable, more accessible and more responsive after major investment periods.
  • Local trust improves when benefits and trade-offs are explained honestly and consistently.

What this means in practice

  • Better local services — more dependable public infrastructure and quicker service upgrades.
  • Stronger communities — safer streets, better shared spaces and more resilient neighbourhoods.
  • Inclusive delivery — accessible design, local participation and fair distribution of investment.
  • Visible accountability — open reporting on impact, progress and outcomes.
