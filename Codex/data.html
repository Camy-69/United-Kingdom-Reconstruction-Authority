Core purpose

  • Bring programme, project and regional data into one common operating picture for decision making.
  • Improve visibility on cost, performance, risk, outcomes and public value.
  • Support decisions across ministries, local authorities, delivery bodies and communities.
  • Make performance easier to understand and compare across places and sectors.

Relevant data streams

  • Programme status — milestones, risk, value and schedule across all major workstreams.
  • Regional insight — place-based conditions, service demand and local delivery performance.
  • Asset performance — condition, resilience and operational output for key infrastructure assets.
  • Public outcomes — jobs, housing quality, environmental improvement and service outcomes.
