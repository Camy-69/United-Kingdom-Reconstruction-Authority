National Standards

Clear, enforceable minimum standards for housing, infrastructure, energy, transport and digital service delivery across the UK.

Mandate

  • Set minimum safety, design quality, resilience and usability requirements for all major works.
  • Ensure housing, transport, energy and public services meet comparable standards across regions.
  • Embed long-term performance, climate resilience and maintenance considerations in design decisions.
  • Maintain a common public standard that supports affordability, inclusion and operational clarity.

Core standards areas

  • Housing & living standards — space, accessibility, energy efficiency, thermal comfort, ventilation and digital readiness.
  • Transport & movement — safer streets, accessible public spaces, active travel infrastructure and robust maintenance standards.
  • Utilities & public services — water, sewerage, digital connectivity, power resilience and service continuity.
  • Energy & climate resilience — heat, decarbonisation, flood adaptation, resilience to extreme weather and life-cycle carbon.
  • Digital & data standards — open data quality, system interoperability, public reporting and cyber resilience.

Delivery and assurance

  • Every major programme must demonstrate compliance against the minimum design and performance baseline.
  • Projects are reviewed on cost, quality, operational fit, accessibility and lifecycle risk.
  • Design decisions are checked against resilience, maintenance and long-term service obligations.
  • Public reporting captures any variance from the approved standards and the mitigation plan.
