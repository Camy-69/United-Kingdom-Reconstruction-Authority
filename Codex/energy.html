Why it matters

  • Reliable energy underpins homes, transport, digital services, industry and national resilience.
  • Net-zero transition must be aligned with supply security, affordability and regional fairness.
  • Decarbonisation needs to be paired with flexibility, storage and local energy resilience.
  • Investment supports improved public-safety and lower long-term operating costs.

Core workstreams

  • Grid modernisation — network upgrades, resilience work, storage and balancing infrastructure.
  • Heat and buildings — low-carbon heating, building-level retrofits and demand reduction.
  • Renewables and generation — solar, offshore and onshore renewables, local generation and flexibility.
  • Energy services — consumer support, smart metering and digital monitoring for better efficiency.
