How procurement works

  • Opportunities are prioritised according to public need, delivery readiness, supply-chain capacity and strategic value.
  • Frameworks and tender routes are designed to balance speed, competition, resilience and long-term operating value.
  • Local inclusion, skills development and social value are tracked alongside price, quality and programme risk.
  • Contract performance is reviewed at milestone, quality and completion stages to improve accountability.

Key data streams

  • Pipeline and award data — scheduled procurements, contract awards, market response and route to market.
  • Supplier and supply chain data — local content, SME engagement, contract concentration and geographic spread.
  • Cost and schedule data — approved budgets, forecast changes, milestone status and commercial risk.
  • Performance and social value data — quality ratings, delivery output, apprenticeship and community benefit metrics.

  • Value for moneyAssured

Governance and reporting

  • Major procurements require public transparency on intent, route to market and evaluation criteria.
  • Commercial assurance reviews test value, risk and delivery readiness before contracts are issued.
  • Contract performance is refreshed in line with milestones, quality checks and stakeholder feedback.
  • Projects with significant variance are escalated to programme boards and published with remediation plans.
