What is published

  • Budget and forecast spend across each programme, region and project.
  • Schedule performance, milestone risks and reasons for delay or change.
  • Quality outcomes, asset condition and service performance after delivery.
  • Community and environmental impact, including local benefit and resilience measures.

Reporting model

  • Programme dataLive
  • Project statusMonthly
  • Performance reviewQuarterly
  • Public updateOpen

Core accountability pillars

  • Money — clear budget, cost variation and delivery value for each programme and project.
  • Time — milestones, delays, risk triggers and recovery actions reported in the open dataset.
  • Quality — asset performance, service reliability and compliance outcomes published in standard format.
  • People and place — public benefit, regional equity, jobs, social value and local lived impact.

Public obligations

  • Budget visibilityMandatory
  • Risk disclosureMandatory
  • Outcomes reportingRequired
  • Decision traceabilityOpen

Why it matters

  • Transparent reporting strengthens public trust by showing how investment decisions are made.
  • It lets ministers, local authorities and communities compare delivery performance without relying on selective updates.
  • It helps identify risk early, improves operational learning and supports faster corrective action.
  • It creates a single evidence base for accountability, public scrutiny and long-term programme management.
