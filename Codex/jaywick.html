Transformation plan

Jaywick & Clacton, Essex

From one of the UK’s most run-down parts of the country to a thriving, connected and sustainable community built around safer homes, greener streets and better local opportunity.

Project snapshot

Key challenges

  • High unemployment and low wages in a structurally challenged seaside economy.
  • Poor quality housing and inefficient heating, drainage and public services.
  • Limited access to public transport, education and local amenities.
  • Climate exposure, flood risk and ageing infrastructure across the coastal edge.

Current major investments

  • Housing regeneration programme£1.1bn
  • Flood and coastal resilience works£790m
  • Transport and public realm upgrades£620m
  • Skills, digital and community services£340m

Our vision

  • Build safer, warmer and more affordable homes close to jobs, schools and public services.
  • Invest in resilient flood protection and accessible public infrastructure.
  • Create new public and green spaces that reconnect neighbourhoods and support healthy living.
  • Expand local training, transport and business opportunity so the area can grow sustainably.
