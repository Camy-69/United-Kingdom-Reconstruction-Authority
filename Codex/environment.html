Programme purpose

  • Reduce climate risk across homes, transport routes, towns, water systems and critical services.
  • Restore degraded landscapes, wetlands, rivers and coastal habitats while improving public amenity.
  • Improve resilience against heat, flood, drought, pollution and ecosystem loss.
  • Ensure environmental investment contributes to long-term social, economic and biodiversity outcomes.

Operational priorities

  • Flood defenceHigh
  • Coastal resilienceHigh
  • BiodiversityHigh
  • Air & water qualityHigh

Core environmental workstreams

  • Flood and coastal resilience — levees, drainage upgrades, floodplain restoration, coastal defence and community warning systems.
  • Water and wastewater resilience — network upgrades, storm overflows reduction, drought preparedness and sustainable water use.
  • Nature recovery and biodiversity — peatland restoration, urban tree planting, habitat creation, river recovery and ecological corridors.
  • Air quality and healthy places — cleaner transport networks, urban greening, dust and emissions reduction and healthier public spaces.
  • Climate adaptation and heat resilience — cooling infrastructure, resilient public realm, heat-risk mapping and adaptation planning.

Delivery and outcomes

  • Projects are prioritised using climate risk, asset condition, social vulnerability and ecological impact scoring.
  • Public investments seek multiple benefits: reduced flood risk, improved health, cleaner environments and stronger local resilience.
  • Nature-based solutions are preferred where they reduce cost and improve long-term performance.
  • Monitoring tracks biodiversity, carbon, flood performance, water quality and public wellbeing indicators.
