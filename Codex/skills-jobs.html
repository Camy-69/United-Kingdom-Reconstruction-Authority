Purpose

  • Ensure the programme has the skilled labour, technical capability and local capacity to deliver major national reconstruction.
  • Match public investment to training, apprenticeships and workforce pipelines in priority sectors.
  • Support local labour markets, improve social mobility and raise productivity in regions most affected by previous underinvestment.
  • Link public works to long-term employment, skills uplift and community benefit.

  • ConstructionHigh

Core workstreams

  • Construction and trades — site management, civil engineering, retrofit, insulation, roofing, plumbing, electrics and maintenance.
  • Utilities and infrastructure — water, energy, sewerage, grid resilience, highways and environmental services.
  • Transport and mobility — rail engineering, active travel design, junction improvement, maintenance and public transport operations.
  • Digital and green skills — data systems, digital delivery, energy retrofit, climate adaptation and low-carbon maintenance.
  • Local economic development — community job matching, training hubs, regional skills clusters and SME participation.

Delivery model

  • Delivery plans will align investment with regional labour market demand, training capacity and apprenticeship availability.
  • Skills pipelines will be built with colleges, local authorities, employers and professional bodies.
  • Contracting and procurement routes will increasingly prioritise training provision, social value and local supply-chain integration.
  • Employment outcomes will be tracked from recruitment through training completion and long-term retention.
