Why it matters

  • Infrastructure underpins housing, public services, industry, transport and resilience for every community.
  • Investment focuses on replacing ageing systems, reducing service failures and improving regional equity.
  • Restoration plans prioritise critical networks first to reduce disruption and improve productivity.
  • Delivery is coordinated across utilities, public services, transport and wider place-based planning.

Core infrastructure workstreams

  • Water and wastewater — mains renewal, leakage reduction, resilience planning and treatment works upgrades.
  • Energy networks — grid reinforcement, local resilience, back-up capacity and decarbonised supply.
  • Digital services — fibre rollout, mobile resilience, secure public data networks and digital inclusion.
  • Local public assets — community facilities, drainage, waste systems and civic service upgrades.
